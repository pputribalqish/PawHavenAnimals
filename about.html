WHO WE ARE

More than just a shelter, we are a place for new beginnings.

Paw Haven aims to provide a welcoming environment where animals receive care and attention while waiting for their forever homes.

Our management system helps administrators organise animal information, monitor adoption applications and maintain important shelter records in one convenient platform.
